Every year, 36 million older adults fall in the United States, and are unable to get the help or medical attention they require for long periods of time. The FallMaster3000 is a cheap (~$35) wristband the size of an eraser that detects falls in real time and instantly alerts emergency contacts or services. We built a fully working prototype for a fraction of the cost of every competitor on the market.

We use the Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense Rev2, 3.7V 300mAh LiPo Battery and TP4056 Charger Module to make a prototype. We were originally going to use Seed Studio XIAO nRF52840 Sense, but due to long shipping times, we were unable to.

Created for the 2026 DEDA Entrepreneuship Competition.
